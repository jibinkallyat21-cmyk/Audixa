Q21 Sales vs VAT returns: if difference, generate reconciliation sheet that explains WHY. Method: code first (period by period vs GL; find GL entries explaining the amount: credit notes/returns, no-VAT entries, neighbouring period entries, manual journals, amounts adding up to difference); AI only for what remains (reads narrations, suggests reason); each reason shown with supporting entries; human confirms; otherwise "unexplained". Accuracy must be proven with worked examples/tests.
Q22 Muqeem: compare only expats (ID starts with 2) in GOSI active contribution sheet with Iqama list from Muqeem (Muqeem has no Saudis; Saudi ID starts with 1); flag both ways and count differences.
Q23 Mudad: compare last month's payroll total with TB salary payable; if mismatch use salary payable ledger to find months pending, booked and paid; detailed monthly reconciliation on one sheet.
Q24 Bank statement to TB ledger: by bank name in ledger name; if two ledgers share a bank, flag for team to pick.
Q25/26 Bank receipts/payments matching: list items with no match within the period (books vs statement, both directions); for each, look in the subsequent statement: cleared after year end = timing difference, flagged and notified to responsible team; not found = unmatched, stronger flag. Only items affecting year end shown. Date tolerance: not given (cheque example implies cleared after year end is the flag).
Q27 Significant/unusual bank transactions: PARKED for later (with Q9).
Q28 Confirmations: prescribed formats exist for all confirmation types, both firms; user supplies later; each confirmation requirement in the client's list has a "download format" option; uploaded confirmation is checked against the format (plus Rule Book bank stamp/signature rule, 4 listed bank confirmation checks).
Q29-31 Cut-off procedure (bank, cash, sales, COGS, expenses): number of transactions is a TEAM SETTING, not preset.
 ABCPA: from the subsequent period statement/ledger: bank = separate numbers for debits and credits (bank charges excluded); other areas = one number each; picked in DATE ORDER (first after year end). Picked items are added to the client's requirement list as sub-requirements under cut-off.
 MISCPA: all five areas: last N transactions before year end and first N after year end (N set by team).
Q32 For every selected cut-off transaction (all areas, both firms): invoice required; short explanation always required even when invoice is provided; if no invoice, explanation is mandatory (accepted only for transactions that genuinely have no invoice). Bank voucher / receipt / transaction receipt never accepted (taken from the same statement).
Reason: invoice date shows the period the cost/income belongs to (unrecorded liability / income).
Q33 Related party/employee names in AR/AP: similar names flagged too (code first, AI for hard cases, person decides). Related set = AOA shareholders+managers, CR directors, the client's own related party list; employees from GOSI documents.
Q34 Write-off list: customers (debit balance) AND customers with credit balance (advances) with no movement in current year; show in two groups.
Q35/36 AR/AP alternative procedures: invoices must equal year-end balance within SAR 1. Lump-sum payments possible: check ledger to see which invoices each payment was booked against; flag to reviewer only if cannot trace. Receipts above balance NOT flagged.
Q37 Advance to suppliers: per firm. MISCPA separate sample list at 50% of its value; ABCPA combined with payables by materiality.
Q38 Related party confirmation: ABCPA gets same confirmation checks as MISCPA (amount vs TB, letterhead, company seal, parent seal where applicable).
Q39 ABCPA related party transactions: above materiality (max 5) plus 3 chosen at random below materiality.
Q40 Fixed assets: compare at the level the TB has (per asset or per class).
Q41 Accrued expenses "schedule preparation stage" = payables found in earlier checks (GOSI payable, VAT payable, salary payable, Zakat and income tax payable).

Q46 "ZATCA compliant documents" criteria: PARKED for later.
Q47/48 Vouching: purchases/COGS/expenses compare supplier name, invoice date, NET amount; invoice number NOT checked. Sales: customer name, invoice date, NET amount, and invoice number still checked.

## Built in the prototype after the Q&A (requirements for the real build)
These are working in `prototype/` with demo data. Treat them as requirements; the demo data generators (`src/utils/client*Data.js`) stand in for reading the client's uploaded files.

- **Audit areas** (file workspace tab). Areas are laid out like the financial statements: Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income and expenses (plus "Unclassified" when the grouping cannot place an account). Area page = the accounts from the trial balance + the procedures. Procedure page = the working tool, a cut-off, or what the client has sent, with a status and the team's notes. The area/procedure list is in `src/utils/auditAreas.js` and must be checked against the Rule Book (Inventory, Related parties, Equity, Accruals, Borrowings, Zakat and tax have notes only).
- **Cut-off.** The team sets the number of items; nothing is preset. ABCPA: from the subsequent period in date order; bank = a number of debits and a number of credits, bank charges left out; other areas = one number. MISCPA: the last N before and the first N after the year end, for bank, sales, cash, cost of sales and expenses. A person approves, then the items go to the client's requirement list. The bank request says a bank voucher or transaction receipt is not accepted (the item was picked from the bank statement); for other areas a short explanation is required even when the document is provided, and mandatory when there is no document.
- **Samples to the client.** After a person approves a sample (sales, purchases, expenses, receivables and payables, materiality method), each item becomes one requirement under its area and sub-group, shown as a table (No., date, ledger, description, reference, amount, status, upload). It can instead be sent as client queries when the nature is an explanation.
- **Materiality method areas (ABCPA).** Revenue, COGS, Expenses take only that area's accounts; Trade receivables and Trade payables use the party list (debit and credit balances). Advances to suppliers are sampled with payables.
- **Bank statements** are a client upload; the cash and bank confirmation tool reads them from the upload instead of asking for typing.
- **Tracking.** The team sees what was sent (who approved, when), what came back, can accept or reject with a reason the client sees, and can send reminders. The number of days before a reminder is set by the team; the prototype default of 3 days is an assumption to confirm.
- **File summary** (Team Lead): where the file stands, what needs attention, samples and cut-off status, and each balance sheet and profit and loss area. Basic documents and statutory data (GOSI, ZATCA, Muqeem, Mudad) are not on it yet.
- **Opening balances** are checked when the trial balance is approved; they are not repeated as a procedure.

## Supporting documents per item (evidence rules), being agreed one area at a time
- **Prepaid expenses.** Each prepaid line needs (A) the agreement or the invoice, whichever shows the amount, the period and the name, and (B) payment proof. Checks against the prepaid schedule line: company name, vendor name, net amount, period start and end. Payment proof = any document that validates the payment (bank statement entry, payment voucher, transfer or SADAD receipt, cheque with clearing, and so on). Status per line: supported and paid / supported, payment not shown / mismatch / not received. Code compares; AI only reads a document it cannot parse; a person decides every flag. (Source: Self-Review, prepaid items 135 and 137; payment proof added by the audit lead.)

## Supporting documents per item (evidence rules)

Agreed with the audit lead, prepaid first; the other areas follow the same pattern and are listed for confirmation.

- Each schedule line is backed by: the agreement (if the item has one), the tax invoice (a government receipt for fee categories outside VAT), and payment proof where the area needs payment.
- The figures (company name, vendor name, net amount, cover start and end) are read from the agreement or the invoice, whichever shows them, and compared with the schedule line.
- Payment proof = anything that validates the payment (bank statement entry, payment voucher, transfer or SADAD receipt, cheque with clearing). It is checked for company, vendor and the amount paid (net plus VAT).
- Status per line: Supported and paid / Supported, payment not shown / Mismatch / Not received. A person decides every line (accept, or raise with the client). Code compares; AI only reads a document code cannot parse.
- Clients upload under any file name; the app shows each document under a standard name: `<Area> - <Item> - <Document type>.<ext>` and keeps the original name beside it.
- Assumption to confirm: agreement applies to Rent and the three insurance categories only.
- Built for prepaid expenses (`src/utils/evidence.js`, `EvidenceDocs.jsx`). Not yet built for other areas; payment proof is not needed for every area.

### Evidence rules: other areas (built, parts are assumptions to confirm)

- Fixed asset additions: invoice (required) and payment proof (required).
- Accruals: contract or engagement letter (if applicable), invoice or statement (required), payment after the year end (required). New tool "Accruals and supporting documents"; it ties the listed accruals to the trial balance.
- Vouching: sales: invoice required, delivery or contract proof and receipt proof if applicable (status "Supported" when no payment proof is needed and none is given). Purchases and expenses: supplier invoice and payment proof required. AR / AP confirmations are unchanged.
- Same matching and statuses as prepaid; every flag is decided by a person.

### ABCPA related party transactions (built)

- Tool under Audit areas, Related parties. ABCPA files only.
- The person chooses how many transactions above and how many below materiality (nothing preset). Materiality is suggested as 0.5% of revenue at 80% and can be typed over.
- Flagged transactions are picked first (round amount, just below materiality, same party/date/amount twice, vague description, close to the year end with a large amount). The weekend (Friday, Saturday) is a weak indicator only. Then the largest above materiality, and a seeded random choice below. Flags are indicators computed by code; an AI reading of descriptions can only explain them, a person decides.
- A picked transaction already sent as a sample or cut-off item from another area is shown as "Already tested in <area> (<item>)" with a link to that exact line in Samples sent / Cut-off sent. It is not asked from the client again. Match = same reference, or same date and amount when no reference.
- Demo: the related party list and the listed transactions stand in for the client's declaration.

## Cash and cash equivalents on one page (built)

- One tool, "Cash and bank accounts", replaces the separate bank statements, bank reconciliation, confirmations and bank / cash cut-off procedures.
- One row per cash or bank account in the trial balance: trial balance figure, confirmation (status and view), statement for the current period (status and view), statement for the subsequent period (status and view), statement closing balance, difference, cut-off progress, status.
- The team asks the client for the documents account by account (confirmation, statement to the year end, statement for the month after). The general bank requests the client has not answered yet are replaced by these. Cash accounts need only a cash count or confirmation.
- A bank reconciliation is requested only for an account whose statement closing balance differs from the trial balance, and it sits inside that account's row (Reconciliation tab). The confirmation checks (six tests) also sit inside the row.
- Cut-off is done per account. The team enters the numbers for each bank (ABCPA: debits and credits; MISCPA: the number before and the same number after the year end). Each bank gets its own block on the client's cut-off list.
- Not carried over: the cash denomination count of the old confirmation tool. Say if it is needed on the cash row.

## Requirement list grouped by audit area (built)

- The client's requirement list is grouped by area, in this order: Cash & Cash Equivalents, Trade Receivables, Inventory, Prepayments & Other Receivables, Fixed Assets, Trade Payables, Accrued Expense & Other Payables, Non Current Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, COGS, Expense, Salary. Trial balance and ledger sit first; anything that fits none sits last under Other. Empty areas are hidden.
- Each area holds its documents, its samples and its cut-off items together. The same grouping is used on the team's Documents view.
- Assumptions to confirm: VAT with Accrued Expense & Other Payables; loans, EOSB and related parties with Non Current Liabilities; staff advances with Prepayments & Other Receivables; rent with Expense.
- The general bank requests are replaced by the per-account ones; whatever the client had already uploaded is carried over, so nothing is asked twice.

## ABCPA materiality setting (built)

- File summary (ABCPA files only) has a Materiality card with "Set materiality". The dialog is the numerical threshold table: basis (sales / revenues 0.5% - 1%, total assets 0.5% - 1%, total equity 1%, income before zakat/tax 5% - 10%), the figure from the approved trial balance, the percentage applied (defaults 1, 0.5, 1, 5; a figure outside the typical range is flagged), and the possible materiality. One basis is chosen ("Yes"); the materiality is that basis at that percentage. A basis at or below zero cannot be chosen.
- The sampling tools and the related party tool use 80% of the saved materiality (the existing ABCPA rule); a figure can still be typed over it for one test. Not applicable to MISCPA files.
- Inside an audit area the sampling tool no longer asks which area is tested: the area is fixed by the procedure opened. The selector stays on the stand-alone sampling page.
- Total equity = share capital + reserves + retained earnings + the year's income; assumption to confirm.

## Bank requests come from the trial balance; supporting documents for the other areas (built)

- When the requirement list is issued, every cash and bank account in the trial balance is requested on its own (confirmation, statement to the year end, statement for the month after; a cash account needs its count sheet). The general bank and cash requests are not issued. There is no separate "ask the client" step. A list issued earlier is brought up to date automatically, carrying over anything already uploaded.
- Supporting documents (same matching and statuses as prepaid; payment proof only where the area needs it; a part payment is accepted where a later receipt or repayment is expected). Assumptions to confirm:
  - Trade receivables: statement of account or confirmation (required); receipt after the year end (if applicable).
  - Trade payables: supplier statement or confirmation (required); payment after the year end (if applicable).
  - Inventory: stock count sheet (required); third-party stock confirmation (if applicable). No payment.
  - Borrowings: loan agreement and lender statement or confirmation (required); repayment proof (if applicable).
  - Related parties: confirmation or statement (required); agreement and payment or receipt proof (if applicable).
  - Equity: registration or articles for capital, shareholder resolution or prior-year statements for reserves and retained earnings (required); proof of capital paid in (if applicable).
  - End of service benefits: computation (required); employment contracts and payments to leavers (if applicable).
- Already built earlier: prepaid, fixed asset additions, accruals, sales / purchases / expenses vouching. Not covered: salary (no ledger line to support; payroll register and WPS proof need their own design), tax and unclassified.

## File summary first, materiality gate, one requirement list, remarks (built)

- "Enter Workspace" opens the file summary. On an ABCPA file with no materiality set, the summary shows a red notice and a "Set materiality" button. The audit areas cannot be opened until materiality is set: opening them sends the person to the file summary with the materiality window already open, and saving it continues to the audit areas. MISCPA files are not gated.
- The audit team's requirement list is now the same as the client's: grouped by audit area, each area holding its documents, samples and cut-off items. The separate "Samples sent" and "Cut-off sent" tabs are gone; the sent, waiting, to-review and overdue counts and the reminder button sit at the top of the list.
- Remarks on every requirement, sample and cut-off item: the client explains what the transaction is, the audit team replies, as a thread. Where the client has no document, "Send as explanation (no document)" sends the explanation alone and the item goes to the team for review (the rule: a short explanation is required even with a document, mandatory without one). Client remarks appear in the audit log and the team's notifications.

- Cut-off requests made before the cut-off was done account by account sat in one all-bank block and repeated the same transactions. They are moved to their own account's block; a transaction asked twice is kept once (the copy the client progressed furthest). This runs automatically when the requirement list opens.

## Laptop screens (built)

- On screens narrower than 1440px the whole app scales down in steps (15px, 14px at 1280px, 13px at 1100px base size), the same effect as zooming the browser out, so every page keeps its layout. The workspace tabs stay on one line. Wide monitors are unchanged.

## IFRS financial statements: who needs them, which type, what the file holds (built)

- The IFRS TB and statements are needed only when the client is engaged for zakat filing (the zakat service on the client profile), for ABCPA and MISCPA files alike; otherwise the page says they are not required. The demo client is set up as engaged for zakat filing so the flow can be tried.
- Only the audit type the client was engaged for is shown: IFRS TB for a proper audit, or for a disclaimer audit, never both.
- The "Financial statements" tab (the MISCPA statutory format) is shown for MISCPA files only.
- Exported file: Client TB, IFRS TB (only ledger name, closing balance of the year, closing balance of the previous year), Balance sheet, P&L, Statement of equity, then the schedules (prepaid, depreciation, EOSB, GOSI expense) and a "Schedule checks" sheet. The working sheets (IFRS TB working columns, mapping, adjustments, capital, COGS, reconciliation, pending list, VAT, verification) follow, because the statements are built from them and every figure must stay traceable. Schedules are in the proper audit file only.
- Every schedule is checked against the trial balance (prepaid, fixed asset cost and accumulated depreciation, depreciation charge, EOSB provision, GOSI expense where the trial balance has a GOSI ledger). A difference is flagged, listed in the pending sheet, and the page offers "Tell <name>": the person who prepared that schedule (from the audit log; otherwise the file's associate) is notified. Nothing is adjusted to force an agreement. Assumption to confirm: who is told for which schedule.

## Samples and cut-off stay, can be added to or deleted; activity in every portal (built)

- Whatever has been sent to the client for a cut-off or a sample shows at the top of that tool every time it is opened ("Already sent to the client"), with a delete button on each item and "Delete all". Deleting an item the client already uploaded against asks for a second confirmation and removes the upload from the list. Samples sent as queries are not removed by this.
- To add more, pick again: the cut-off tool no longer offers transactions that were already sent ("Pick more items"), and the sampling tools never send an item twice.
- The audit log is one shared log, shown newest first by the time of the event everywhere. Every portal now has it: the client (Activity Log), audit team and manager (Audit Log), front office (new Audit Log page), management (new "Audit trail" tab on its Audit Log). Recent activity from the same log is on the audit team, client, manager, front office and management dashboards.

## Audit areas: one place for documents, sampling and vouching and cut-off apart; related parties on their own (built)

- Each area is arranged the same way: what can be done together is one procedure (the schedule or documents of the area, with the supporting documents inside it), then Sampling, then Vouching of the samples, then Cut-off, each its own procedure where the area has them. Cash and cash equivalents: accounts (documents, confirmations, statements, reconciliation) and cut-off. Trade receivables and payables: supporting documents, sampling and confirmations, vouching. Revenue, cost of sales, expenses: sampling, vouching, cut-off. Prepayments, fixed assets, accruals: the schedule and its supporting documents. Payroll: salary, GOSI, end of service and supporting documents in one tool. Borrowings, inventory, equity: supporting documents. The separate "notes" procedures of those areas are gone; each procedure page already has a notes and conclusion box. Zakat and tax and unclassified accounts keep their notes procedure.
- Related parties are separate: their own section in the audit areas (Supporting documents, Sampling of transactions, Vouching) and their own group ("Related Parties", after Non Current Liabilities) in the requirement list, for the client and for the audit team.

## Audit areas flow: visual tidy (built)

- Same look, made consistent across the area list, area pages and procedure pages: one icon per kind of work (sampling, vouching, cut-off, documents, accounts), one status chip (Not started, In progress, Complete) in place of two, no repeated headings inside a procedure page, readable section titles and hints, evidence status chips in the app's colours, the cash and bank table kept inside the page width, and the footer at the bottom of the page.
